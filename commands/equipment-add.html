Equipment addition

equipment add name/NAME category/CATEGORY condition/CONDITION [notes/NOTES]

The brackets mark optional input; do not type them. Arguments may appear in any order, separated by spaces or tabs. Names and categories are trimmed, required, and may contain punctuation (for example name/Canon EOS-R50 (kit)). Notes are trimmed and default to an empty string, including when notes/ is explicitly empty. Conditions are GOOD, FAIR, DAMAGED, and UNDER_REPAIR, ignoring case; under repair and under-repair are also accepted.

Missing or blank required fields, repeated parameters (including notes), unknown parameters, and text before the first parameter are rejected. Whitespace-delimited or start-of-input tokens matching [A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9_-]*/ are reserved markers, even within notes. There is no quoting or escaping. URLs such as https://example.com and ordinary internal slashes remain text.

Each execution generates a UUID. Identical descriptions may represent different physical units and receive separate identities. A generated UUID collision fails without changing any collection; retry the command. GOOD/FAIR items begin AVAILABLE; DAMAGED/UNDER_REPAIR items begin UNAVAILABLE. Availability comes from the model and is never persisted as a separate field. Success identifies the item, UUID and availability and requests the new equipment's details screen.

EquipmentCard(Equipment, Availability, Optional<Member>) renders a snapshot of name, category, full UUID, condition, availability and holder name/NUS ID. An unresolved holder on ASSIGNED equipment is explicitly marked as a missing reference. Its fields wrap at narrow widths; its parent owns selection and callbacks.

Integration boundary

This contribution supplies EquipmentAddCommand, EquipmentAddCommandParser and EquipmentCard; it does not activate the family router or assemble the application shell. At baseline 97ee5c3370fc517cdd3586c25b0ad0f08f874c22, equipment add still routes to a placeholder. Duy must route it to the individual parser and consume the card in the equipment list. Keith must consume the typed display request in the shell. Full command execution through LogicManager, automatic saving, and visible selection/details must be verified after those integrations land.

Direct parser/command integration tests use a real ModelManager and temporary-file JsonAddressBookStorage. They verify all four conditions, IDs, notes and derived availability after reload. Saving remains the responsibility of LogicManager; commands never write storage. The existing pipeline does not roll back in-memory changes when saving fails, and this feature does not promise such rollback.
