Equipment addition

equipment add name/NAME category/CATEGORY condition/CONDITION [notes/NOTES]

Parameters may appear in any order. Name/category/condition are required; notes may be omitted or explicitly empty. Names allow 1-100 printable Unicode characters, categories 1-50 and notes at most 300. Text whitespace is normalized; notes line breaks become spaces. Conditions accept exactly GOOD, FAIR, DAMAGED and UNDER_REPAIR, ignoring case. under repair and under-repair are invalid. See MVP fields for complete validation and first-error rules.

UUIDs are generated at execution, using random version 4 identifiers normally. Identical descriptions are distinct physical units. Existing UUID collisions are silently retried, with a 100-attempt safeguard for a pathological generator. Failure leaves existing equipment unchanged; only a successful addition is saved. Feedback and the typed display request contain the actual new UUID.

GOOD/FAIR items begin AVAILABLE; DAMAGED/UNDER_REPAIR items begin UNAVAILABLE. Availability is derived from condition and open loans, never persisted as an extra field. Success selects the new row and opens the existing details panel. The card preserves the complete UUID, condition, availability and holder.

The route and real shell are integrated through merged #95/#93. LogicManager stages the addition, atomically saves and then commits through the live root. A failed save does not create an in-memory item and cannot be persisted by a later command. Individual commands/cards do not write storage.
